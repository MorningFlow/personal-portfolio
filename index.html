Project: Automation01 / 07
I built a system that turns happy customers into public reviews — automatically.
AI Customer Feedback & Reputation System. WhatsApp automation.
The Problem02 / 07
Happy customers leave. Unhappy ones leave reviews.
No way to reach satisfied customers after the sale — while dissatisfied ones went straight to Google.
The Insight03 / 07
More public praise. Fewer public complaints.
Route satisfied customers to Google Reviews. Catch dissatisfied ones privately. The logic is simple. The timing is everything.
The Flow04 / 07
Invoice first. Feedback second.
Every purchase triggers a WhatsApp message delivering the invoice. A follow-up asks for a star rating — at the moment attention is highest.
The Logic05 / 07
Customer rates their experience
4 – 5 stars
→ Google Reviews
Direct link to leave a public review while the experience is fresh.
1 – 3 stars
→ Private capture
Feedback stays internal. Business can resolve — not broadcast.
The Challenge06 / 07
The billing software didn't have a webhook.
The trigger had to be built manually — intercepting the invoice event and passing it into the WhatsApp sequence without a native integration.
Result07 / 07
More public praise. Fewer public complaints.
Positive reviews increased. Dissatisfied customers caught privately. Zero extra staff effort.

Project: Infrastructure01 / 07
I built the infrastructure to send thousands of emails without getting flagged as spam.
Cold email infrastructure and outreach automation.
The Problem02 / 07
A large lead list. One email sent at a time.
Outreach was manual and slow — sending in bulk without infrastructure risked landing every email in spam.
The Insight03 / 07
Volume isn't the hard part. Deliverability is.
Sending thousands of emails is trivial. Getting them into inboxes instead of spam folders is the actual engineering problem.
System04 / 07
  • Domain strategy
  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC
  • Warm-up infrastructure
  • Bounce protection
  • Volume ramp-up
Multiple sending domains, authenticated and warmed before a single campaign email went out.
The Personalization05 / 07
Every email reads like it was written for one person.
First name, company, job title, and custom variables pulled straight from a CSV list — so campaigns never felt like a mail merge.
The Sequence06 / 07
1Open
2Follow up
3Follow up
4Follow up
5Break-up
"A five-email sequence, ending in a deliberate goodbye — not a fade-out."
Result07 / 07
From one-by-one to thousands, without losing the inbox.
Manual outreach became a scalable system sending high-volume personalized campaigns with strong inbox placement.
Safe at scale
