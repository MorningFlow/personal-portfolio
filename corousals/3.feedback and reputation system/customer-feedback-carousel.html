Project: Automation01 / 07
I built a system that turns happy customers into public reviews — automatically.
AI Customer Feedback & Reputation System. WhatsApp automation for a tyre shop.
The Problem02 / 07
Happy customers leave. Unhappy ones leave reviews.
The business had no way to reach satisfied customers after the sale — while dissatisfied ones went straight to Google.
The Insight03 / 07
More public praise. Fewer public complaints.
Route satisfied customers to Google Reviews. Catch dissatisfied ones privately before they post. The logic is simple. The timing is everything.
The Flow04 / 07
Invoice first. Feedback second.
Every purchase triggers a WhatsApp message delivering the invoice. A follow-up asks for a star rating — the moment the customer's attention is highest.
The Logic05 / 07
Customer rates their experience ★★★★★ or less
4 – 5 stars
→ Google Reviews
Sent a direct link to leave a public review while the experience is still fresh.
1 – 3 stars
→ Private capture
Feedback stays internal. The business can follow up and resolve — not broadcast.
The Challenge06 / 07
The billing software didn't have a webhook.
The trigger had to be built manually — intercepting the invoice event and passing it into the WhatsApp sequence without a native integration. The routing logic had to be bulletproof: a misfire sends a 3-star customer to Google.
Result07 / 07
More public praise. Fewer public complaints.
Positive reviews increased, dissatisfied customers were caught privately, and the business gained a steady stream of real feedback — with zero extra effort from staff.
