## Slide-by-Slide Copy

### 01 — Cover
- Eyebrow: `PROJECT: INFRASTRUCTURE`
- Dominant (headline): *"I built the infrastructure to send thousands of emails without getting flagged as spam."*
- Subordinate: *Cold email infrastructure and outreach automation.*

### 02 — The Problem
- Eyebrow: `THE PROBLEM`
- Dominant: *"A large lead list. One email sent at a time."*
- Subordinate: outreach was manual and slow, and sending in bulk without proper infrastructure risked landing every email in spam.

### 03 — The Insight
- Eyebrow: `THE INSIGHT`
- Dominant: *"Volume isn't the hard part. Deliverability is."*
- Subordinate: sending thousands of emails is trivial. Getting them into inboxes instead of spam folders is the actual engineering problem.

### 04 — What I Built
- Eyebrow: `SYSTEM`
- Dominant (stacked list): *Domain strategy / SPF, DKIM, DMARC / Warm-up infrastructure / Bounce protection / Volume ramp-up*
- Subordinate: *Multiple sending domains, authenticated and warmed, before a single campaign email went out.*

### 05 — The Personalization
- Eyebrow: `THE PERSONALIZATION`
- Dominant: *"Every email reads like it was written for one person."*
- Subordinate: first name, company, job title, and custom variables pulled straight from a CSV list, so campaigns never felt like a mail merge.

### 06 — The Architecture
- Eyebrow: `THE SEQUENCE`
- Dominant (5 numbered steps): *Open, Follow up, Follow up, Follow up, Break-up*
- Subordinate: *"A five-email sequence, ending in a deliberate goodbye, not a fade-out."*

### 07 — Outcome
- Eyebrow: `RESULT`
- Dominant: *"From one-by-one to thousands, without losing the inbox."*
- Subordinate: manual outreach became a scalable system, sending high-volume personalized campaigns while keeping strong inbox placement.
- Accent: muted violet tag pill — `SAFE AT SCALE`
