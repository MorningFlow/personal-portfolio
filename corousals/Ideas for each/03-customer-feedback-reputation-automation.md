# Carousel Ideation — Customer Feedback & Reputation Automation

**Card / Accent:** Card 3 — sage green (`#5dab78`)
**Slide Count:** 7
**Status:** Copy locked (renamed from "AI-Powered Customer Feedback & Google Review Automation" — no AI involved, straightforward rule-based automation). Awaiting real outcome number (optional) and screenshots.

---

## Slide-by-Slide Copy

### 01 — Cover
- Eyebrow: `PROJECT: AUTOMATION`
- Dominant (headline): *"I built a system that only asks happy customers to go public."*
- Subordinate: *Customer Feedback & Reputation Automation. Built for a tire retailer.*

### 02 — The Problem
- Eyebrow: `THE PROBLEM`
- Dominant: *"Bonuses depended on reviews. Nobody asked for them."*
- Subordinate: customer numbers already existed in the billing system, but there was no post-purchase contact and no appetite for spammy marketing texts.

### 03 — The Insight
- Eyebrow: `THE INSIGHT`
- Dominant: *"Give value first. Ask for the review second."*
- Subordinate: instead of a cold "please review us" message, the invoice itself became the first touchpoint, delivered instantly and usefully, before any ask was made.

### 04 — The Customer Flow
- Eyebrow: `THE FLOW`
- Dominant (4 numbered steps): *Purchase, Invoice, Delivered, Asked*
- Subordinate: *"Buy tires. Get your invoice on WhatsApp. Then one question: how was it?"*

### 05 — The Logic (centerpiece slide)
- Eyebrow: `THE ROUTING`
- Dominant (two-branch split, stacked lines with weight contrast):
  *5 stars → sent to Google, encouraged to post*
  *4 or below → sent to a private form, kept internal*
- Subordinate: *"Happy customers go public. Everyone else gets heard, quietly."*
- Accent: sage green rule separating the two branches — this is the single slide that carries the deck

### 06 — The Hard Part
- Eyebrow: `THE CHALLENGE`
- Dominant: *"WhatsApp's API wasn't built for this."*
- Subordinate: the Business API's limitations meant building webhook-driven integrations between the billing software and third-party services just to keep the experience feeling instant and seamless.

### 07 — Outcome
- Eyebrow: `RESULT`
- Dominant: *"More public praise. Fewer public complaints."*
- Subordinate: positive reviews increased, dissatisfied customers were caught privately instead of publicly, and the business gained a steady stream of real feedback, with zero extra effort from staff.
- Accent: sage green tag pill — `ZERO STAFF EFFORT`
